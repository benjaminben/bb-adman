# Assumptions made to keep the build moving

Each assumption the build makes while a question is open, with how hard it is to undo. The open questions and what waits on the stakeholder are in `spec/open-items.md`. When an item is decided, the decision goes into `DECISIONS.md` and the topic file it changes, and the item is removed from here; new assumptions are added here as they are made.

`OI-xx` numbers are kept from the old open-items table. `Q-0xx` numbers come from the old question list. `A-xx` marks a question that never had a number. "Change later" says how hard it is to undo the assumption: easy, some rework, or hard.
