# Custom ComfyUI workflows: the slices

The build of `spec/comfyui-custom.md` (D-104), cut into slices CW-1 to CW-8. Every developer reads `spec/phases/DEV-RULES.md` first, then the parts of `spec/comfyui-custom.md` the slice names. Each slice is sized to finish well under 300k tokens. Verification classes are those of `spec/testing.md`, "Verification classes"; the full checks run after each merge, as the merge rule says.

## Order and lanes

| Wave | Slices, side by side | Waits for |
|------|----------------------|-----------|
| 1 | CW-1 | nothing |
| 2 | CW-2 | CW-1 |
| 3 | CW-3, CW-4 | CW-2 |
| 4 | CW-5, CW-6a, CW-7 | CW-3 |
| 5 | CW-6b | CW-6a |
| 6 | CW-8 | every other CW slice |

- At most three lanes at once (D-060). One merge at a time.
- **Review.** Full: CW-1, CW-2, CW-3, CW-6a, CW-8. Short: CW-4, CW-5, CW-6b, CW-7. Every slice that edits an existing test is reviewed (D-074).
- **Shots.** Picture numbers 520 to 539. CW-4 uses 520 to 525, CW-5 526 to 529, CW-6b 530 to 533, CW-7 534 to 535.
- **The real ComfyUI server.** Only CW-8 runs one proof on it. Every other slice tests against the stand-in.
